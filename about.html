About

Kassian Rodriguez is a writer and researcher based in Oakland, California. He is a recovering particle physicist, with contributions to the Fermi-LAT and ATLAS collaborations at UC Santa Cruz, and the Weinberg Theory Institute at UT Austin, where he received his Master's in physics. His writing focuses on the human labor, cultural assumptions, and ambient theology beneath contemporary AI development.

He is at work on a series of essays and reported features about the technology industry. For inquiries about writing, research, or collaboration, please reach out by email.
