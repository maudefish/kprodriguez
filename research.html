Theory and Application of Chiral Anomalies in Quantum Field Theory

Master's Thesis · University of Texas at Austin · 2023

An investigation of the emergence of chiral anomalies in quantum field theories, as well as their implications for physical observables. Derives the U(1) axial anomaly via the path integral formalism and applies the result to calculate the lifetime of the η′ meson to within 2σ of experimental values reported by the Particle Data Group.

Optimizing Operator-Task Assignments via Linear Programming

Whitepaper · 2025

An operations research (OR) model using mixed-integer linear programming for maximizing data volume and quality in data collection for autonomous systems. Introduces a quality-scaled rate metric that jointly optimizes for operator speed and accuracy, yielding a 62.5% improvement over random assignment and 13% over the best greedy heuristic across 10,000 Monte Carlo simulations.

Search for CP Violating Gamma-Ray Signals with Fermi-LAT Pass 8 Data

Bachelor's Thesis · University of California, Santa Cruz · 2018

A search for signatures of Charge-Parity (CP) violation imprinted on primordial extragalactic magnetic fields using 10 years of Fermi-LAT gamma-ray data. Constructs a parity-odd correlator from gamma-ray triplets to probe magnetic helicity via AGN-sourced cascades, with statistical significance assessed through 500 Monte Carlo simulations. Reports a null result at 95% containment, constraining prior claims of a left-handed signal.

Teleoperation Receiver Dongle Mounting Height

Standard Operating Procedure · KPR-SOP-001 Rev A · 2026

A controlled procedure written to replace an informal fix on a robotics floor, where a Bluetooth receiver taped to a metal chassis sat at a field node and dropped the link. Derives the quarter-wave mounting height from the boundary condition, specifies it with a tolerance (31 ± 1 mm), bounds where it applies (working ranges under 2 m), and gives an acceptance check, an escalation path, and stated limitations. Prepared as a sample of procedure writing, outside any employer's document-control system.
